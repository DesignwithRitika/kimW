Kim Witczak — Healthcare Advocacy Visual Design
Transforming information into engaging experiences through clear, credible, and consistent visual communication.

Project Overview
For over a year, I worked as the lead graphic designer supporting Kim Witczak through Usharo Media Pvt. Ltd. Kim's work focuses on pharmaceutical safety and patient advocacy, including educating audiences about antidepressant risks, pharmaceutical advertising practices, and informed healthcare decisions.
The design challenge was to make complex healthcare information easier to understand and educational content more engaging—without compromising credibility.

Project Details
- Client: Kim Witczak
- Industry: Healthcare advocacy and awareness
- Engagement: Over one year
- Role: Lead Graphic Designer
- Agency: Usharo Media Pvt. Ltd.

The Challenge
The project materials identified several opportunities to improve the existing visual communication:
- Generic, text-heavy visuals with limited engagement
- Thumbnails with limited visual storytelling and context
- Complex medical information that was difficult to digest
- Inconsistent creative direction across platforms
- A need to improve audience retention while maintaining credibility
